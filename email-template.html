THE ART OF PROFESSIONAL EMAIL

Small message,
lasting impression.

A well-written email respects the recipient's time, communicates its purpose clearly and maintains a professional tone.

Email is an important communication tool in academic and professional environments. Students may use it to contact lecturers, ask questions, submit documents or enquire about career opportunities.

As a Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student, I want to communicate confidently and respectfully with potential employers. This page presents an example of an email I could adapt when enquiring about a Data Analyst Intern or industrial attachment opportunity.

01 / EMAIL EXAMPLE

A professional enquiry.

Use this example as a guide. Replace the placeholders and adjust the message to suit the actual recipient and opportunity.

Professional email example

✉
TO

bocra@gmail.com

FROM

carolmothobi4@gmail.com

SUBJECT

Enquiry About Data Analyst Industrial Attachment Opportunity

Dear Hiring Manager,

I hope this email finds you well.

My name is Carol Mothobi, and I am a first-year Bachelor of Science in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student at Botswana Accountancy College.

I am writing to enquire whether your organisation offers industrial attachment or internship opportunities in Data Analytics or a related field. I am interested in gaining practical experience, learning from experienced professionals and developing my understanding of how data supports business decision-making.

My current computer skills include Microsoft Word, Microsoft Access and basic Microsoft Excel. I am eager to continue developing my technical abilities and contribute positively to assigned tasks while learning from your team.

If opportunities are available, I would be grateful for information about the application process, eligibility requirements and any supporting documents I should provide. I would also appreciate guidance on the appropriate person or department to contact.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,

Carol Mothobi
Bachelor of Science in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics Student
Botswana Accountancy College
Telephone: 75156162
Email: carolmothobi4@gmail.com

Clear. Courteous. Professional.

02 / KNOW THE STRUCTURE

The essentials of
a polished email.

01

Subject Line

Summarise the purpose of the message so that the recipient immediately understands what the email concerns.

Example

Enquiry About Industrial Attachment

02

Professional Greeting

Address the recipient respectfully. Use their name or title when known, or a suitable greeting such as "Dear Hiring Manager".

Example

Dear Ms. Kgabo,

03

Clear Purpose

Introduce yourself briefly and explain why you are contacting the recipient. Keep the message focused on the reason for writing.

Example

I am writing to enquire about...

04

Polite Closing

Thank the recipient for their time and end with a courteous closing followed by your name and appropriate contact details.

Example

Kind regards, Carol Mothobi

03 / EMAIL ETIQUETTE

The little details
matter.

Good email etiquette helps build a positive professional impression.

♡ Do this

  • ✓ Use a clear and relevant subject line.
  • ✓ Write in complete sentences with correct grammar.
  • ✓ Use a respectful and professional tone.
  • ✓ Keep the message concise and organised.
  • ✓ Check recipient details before sending.
  • ✓ Attach documents when requested and mention them.

✕ Avoid this

  • ✕ Using vague subjects such as "Hi" or "Help".
  • ✕ Writing the entire message in capital letters.
  • ✕ Using excessive slang or emojis in formal emails.
  • ✕ Sending a message without proofreading it.
  • ✕ Forgetting to include your name and contact details.
  • ✕ Following up repeatedly within a very short time.

04 / FOLLOWING UP

Professional communication
doesn't end at send.

If an employer has not responded, allow a reasonable amount of time before sending a polite follow-up. Refer briefly to your earlier message, restate your enquiry and thank the recipient for their time.

Example subject: Follow-up on Industrial Attachment Enquiry

Dear Hiring Manager,

I hope you are well. I am following up on my previous email regarding potential Data Analyst industrial attachment opportunities. I would appreciate any update or guidance you may be able to provide.

Thank you again for your time and consideration.

Kind regards,
Carol Mothobi

As I progress in my studies and prepare for industrial attachment, I aim to become more confident in writing professional emails, asking thoughtful questions and building positive professional relationships.
