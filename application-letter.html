BEFORE SENDING

Make it application-ready.

A few final checks can help you present a professional and relevant application to a potential employer.

01

Personalise the recipient

Replace the placeholders with the actual company name, address, date and recipient details. Use a specific hiring manager's name if known.

02

Match the opportunity

Review the internship advertisement and adjust your letter to reflect the employer's actual requirements and responsibilities.

03

Attach your CV

Include an up-to-date CV and any other documents requested by the employer. Make sure your contact details are correct.

04

Review before sending

Check spelling, grammar, formatting and the accuracy of your claims. Save the final letter as a PDF when submitting it professionally.
