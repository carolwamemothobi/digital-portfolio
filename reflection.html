My Academic Learning Journey: Growing Beyond My Comfort Zone

By Carol Mothobi ♡ First-Year Student

Starting my journey at Botswana Accountancy College has introduced me to a new chapter of learning, responsibility and personal development. As a first-year student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics, I am beginning to understand that tertiary education is not only about passing examinations but also about developing the knowledge, discipline and confidence needed for the future.

Adjusting to a New Academic Environment

Moving from secondary school to tertiary education requires me to take greater responsibility for my learning. I am learning that success involves more than attending classes and completing assignments. It also requires independent study, effective time management, asking questions and taking initiative when I do not understand something. Adjusting to these expectations is an ongoing process, and I recognise the importance of developing consistent study habits.

One lesson I am taking from this transition is that I cannot rely on motivation alone. Planning my tasks, breaking larger assignments into smaller steps and reviewing concepts regularly can help me remain organised. I also understand that seeking clarification when I need help is a positive step towards learning rather than a sign of weakness.

Discovering My Academic Interests

My programme has strengthened my interest in the relationship between business, technology and data. I am particularly interested in how Business Intelligence and Data Analytics can help organisations understand information, identify patterns and make better decisions. This field appeals to my curiosity and interest in analytical thinking and problem-solving.

I know that becoming proficient in this area will require patience and continuous practice. I want to improve my technical abilities, strengthen my understanding of analytical concepts and become more confident when working with data. I also hope to connect what I learn in class with practical situations and real organisational needs.

Challenges, Growth and Self-Improvement

I recognise that balancing different academic responsibilities can be challenging. New concepts may take time to understand, and assignments require careful planning and effort. Rather than expecting myself to understand everything immediately, I am learning to approach difficulties with patience, persistence and an open mind.

I can respond to these challenges by organising my schedule, practising regularly, using appropriate learning resources and collaborating respectfully with classmates. Feedback is also valuable because it helps me identify areas where I need to improve. I want to become more adaptable and confident when facing unfamiliar tasks.

Looking Towards the Future

My long-term goal is to establish a successful career in the technology industry, particularly in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics. To work towards this ambition, I intend to remain committed to my studies, develop practical skills and seek opportunities to gain industry experience. I also want to strengthen my communication, teamwork and problem-solving abilities because these skills are important in professional settings.

Overall, my academic journey is teaching me to view learning as a continuous process. I may still be at the beginning, but every lesson gives me an opportunity to improve. I want to remain curious, learn from challenges and celebrate progress without losing sight of my goals. My success will not be defined only by the qualifications I obtain, but also by the knowledge, character and abilities I develop along the way.
