HELLO, I'M CAROL

Ambitious, curious
and always growing.

My name is Carol Mothobi, and I am a first-year student at Botswana Accountancy College, pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics.

I would describe myself as ambitious, hardworking and curious. I enjoy learning new things, exploring different ideas and taking on challenges that help me become a better version of myself. I believe that growth requires patience, effort and the willingness to learn from both success and failure.

I approach my academic journey with an open mind and a desire to understand how things work. Whether I am learning a new concept, solving a problem or working with others, I see each experience as an opportunity to develop knowledge and confidence.

I am still discovering all that I am capable of, but I am excited about the possibilities ahead. My journey is one of learning, self-discovery and steadily working towards the future I envision.

With ambition and grace, Carol ♡
