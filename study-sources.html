A LITTLE STUDY INSPIRATION

Learning is my glow-up.

As a first-year Bachelor of Science in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student at Botswana Accountancy College, I understand that success requires consistency, curiosity and a willingness to keep learning.

My course involves developing an understanding of technology, mathematics, statistics, business and data. I want to strengthen my knowledge by practising regularly, exploring credible academic resources and connecting classroom concepts to practical situations.

This page brings together useful study habits and learning platforms that I can use throughout my academic journey. The resources are learning recommendations, and I will continue building my own routine as I discover what works best for me.

MY STUDY STRATEGY

Habits that help me stay on track.

Small, consistent habits can make studying more organised and manageable.

♡

Plan my time

I can use a weekly timetable to organise lectures, assignments, revision and personal commitments. Breaking large tasks into smaller steps helps me work towards deadlines without leaving everything until the last minute.

✧

Take useful notes

I can summarise important concepts in my own words, highlight key definitions and write down questions that need further explanation. Organised notes make revision easier.

♕

Practise regularly

Subjects involving mathematics, statistics and data require practice. Working through exercises and reviewing mistakes can help me understand concepts rather than memorising answers.

♡

Prepare for assessments

I can review learning outcomes, practise past questions when available and identify topics that need extra attention. Starting early gives me time to ask for help when necessary.

✦

Limit distractions

Studying in a suitable environment, silencing unnecessary notifications and taking short breaks can help me maintain concentration and avoid unnecessary stress.

✧

Reflect and improve

After a test or assignment, I can review feedback, recognise what went well and make a plan to improve areas where I struggled. Progress comes from learning from mistakes.

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

Research with purpose.

Finding information is only one part of research. I also need to evaluate the quality of the information, understand what it says and acknowledge the work of other authors.

  • Use focused keywords: Search for terms that directly relate to the assignment question.
  • Check the source: Consider the author's qualifications, the publication, the date and the evidence provided.
  • Compare information: Consult more than one suitable source instead of relying on a single article or website.
  • Keep research notes: Record the title, author, publication date, URL and other details needed for referencing.
  • Reference correctly: Follow the referencing style required by my lecturer and distinguish my own ideas from those of other authors.
  • Paraphrase responsibly: Explain ideas in my own words while preserving their meaning and providing appropriate citations.

5–10 min
